# Game content

Story Director is an unofficial fan tool. SpongeBob SquarePants and Battle for Bikini Bottom – Rehydrated belong to their respective rights holders.

You need your own installed copy of the game. The catalog contains asset identifiers and scene locations; the game loads the content locally. No game executables, levels, textures, audio, saves or dialogue transcripts are included.
